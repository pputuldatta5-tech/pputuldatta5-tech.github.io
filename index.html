THE OBJECTIVE

Reduce repetitive first-draft work by using lead context to create a relevant B2B outreach draft.

WHAT I BUILT

Connected a lead sheet to an n8n workflow, passed lead details into an AI prompt, and mapped the generated subject and message into a Gmail draft.

WHAT IT DEMONSTRATES

Data flow, prompt design, workflow orchestration, lead handling, and human-in-the-loop review.
